# Living Mathematics: Fibonacci, Emergence, and Regenerative Growth

Companion educational chapter to the Founder's Covenant (AIFT-FC-001). This does not amend or paraphrase the Covenant.

Fibonacci numbers satisfy F(0)=0, F(1)=1, F(n)=F(n-1)+F(n-2). The ratio of consecutive positive terms approaches φ=(1+√5)/2 ≈ 1.6180339887. The golden angle is 360°/φ² ≈ 137.507764°. Botanical phyllotaxis sometimes exhibits neighboring Fibonacci spiral counts, but not every natural spiral follows Fibonacci patterns; a nautilus shell is not proof of a golden spiral.

The Tree of Emergence is an interpretive correspondence: roots = inheritance; trunk = shared responsibility; branches = diverse sovereign paths; leaves = learning; fruits = contributions; seeds = future possibilities. These meanings are metaphors, not mathematical laws of governance or guaranteed economic returns.
