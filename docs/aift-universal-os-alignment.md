## Future technical claims: evidence required

- **Native OS:** exact Linux/boot image and QEMU or approved hardware traces, not a philosophical naming convention.
- **Hosted OS:** authenticated isolated session, persistent state, verifiable service contract and denial tests.
- **Mac/Windows:** model-specific verified compatible boot chain; an EFI interface is not a replacement BIOS.
- **Embodied AI:** ROS 2 simulation and independent safety controls; software experimentation is not functional-safety certification.
- **Human sovereignty:** revocable permissions and privacy-preserving, exportable agent memory must be observable and tested, not just promised.

## Research handoffs

Any engineering proposal that uses concepts from the Scroll, governance, identity, finance, safety or medicine should cite exact text and identify the proposition vs measured implementation. Reconcile disputed philosophical claims as research rather than silently rewriting canonical doctrine. Physical robotics and smartphone deployment require distinct platform safety and consent review.
